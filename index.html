VR Arcade - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy is for the app "VR Arcade" for Meta Quest, made by Hatesh (Your Neighbourhood QA) ("we", "us"). It explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how you can ask for it to be deleted.

1. What data the app collects

The app does not collect your personal data and does not send any data to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no online features and no tracking. It does not use your microphone, camera pictures or your location.

The app saves a small amount of information only on your headset, so that the game works the way you left it:

  • Your high scores, level progress and star ratings.
  • Your settings: volume, music, turning, comfort options, text size, seated mode, and similar choices.
  • A small log file with game events and error messages (for example the name of a scene or an error), plus the headset model, system version, graphics chip and app version. It does not contain your name, your account, or the name of your device. The log is kept on your headset and is only shared if you choose to copy the file and send it to us yourself.

The app uses hand and controller tracking (positions of your head, hands and fingers) while you play, so that you can point, grab and swing. This is handled in real time by your headset and the game. We do not record, store or send it.

2. How the data is used

The information saved on your headset is used only to run the app: to show your scores, to remember your settings, and, if you share it with us, to find and fix problems. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing with others

We do not share any data with other people or companies, because we do not receive any. Meta's own systems on your headset (such as the Meta Horizon Store and Meta's tracking features) work under Meta's privacy policies, which apply separately to the app.

4. How long the data is kept and how to delete it

Everything the app saves stays on your headset. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's data in your headset's storage settings. Because we do not receive or store your data on our side, there is nothing on our servers to delete. If you sent us a log file and want it deleted, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

5. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Please follow Meta's age rules for Meta Quest.

6. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date before the change goes live.
